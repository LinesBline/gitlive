self-sovereign deploy

Your software.
Your hardware.
Your rules.

A git push becomes a live process on hardware you control — signed, gated and audited. No platform in between, nothing leaving this machine.

  • Push is the deploy. Owner-signed manifests, gated installs, receipts for every change.
  • Names you own. Local names, your domains, certificates and the mesh — no naming zone over you.
  • Nothing phones home. No telemetry, no third parties. Your session lives on your disk, scrypt-hashed.
