What gitlive is — and is not

  • Is: a resilience system. No company, registry, or government that pressures one of them can kill your project by taking down one thing.
  • Is not: an anonymity system, and not an escape hatch from the law. Hardening phases are explicit; nothing claims anti-takedown before they ship.
  • Honest status: deploys, backend layer, repo-as-storage, mesh deploy + failover, control-plane dashboard — working and test-green today. Federation protocol, at-rest encryption policies, public onboarding — in active build (DESIGN.md in the repo is the source of truth).
